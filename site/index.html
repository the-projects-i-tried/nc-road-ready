THE FINE PRINT, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Practice you can inspect.

What this is

An independent North Carolina driving-knowledge practice game, not an NCDOT or NCDMV product. Questions are original practice material, not an official exam question bank. Signs and road-rule questions are mixed for learning; real sign recognition is a separate testing component.

Content status: please read

The full handbook URL repeatedly timed out during this build. Its entries were adapted from the preceding handbook-based quiz conversation, not independently rechecked against the current PDF. No edition, page numbers, or “verified today” label has been invented. Source sections are navigation aids until checked.

The separate official regulatory- and warning-sign sheets were accessible and inspected. Those questions are distinguished as “Source checked.” All imported packs start unverified. The source badges are not a substitute for reading the current handbook or following current law, road signs, and officers’ instructions.

What “adaptive” means here

The next question is selected using this page’s answers: mistakes increase a rule’s selection weight, recent rules get a cooldown, and new variants are preferred. Adaptive difficulty responds to recent results. “Practiced” requires two unassisted successes on different variants separated by other questions. No score is a calibrated prediction of passing a driving exam.

Not infinite—and no made-up numbers

Reordering choices does not count as a new question. Asking for a narrow topic can exhaust its variants. The app says so and offers another topic, an imported pack, or explicitly allowed review. It never silently manufactures a new legal rule.

Privacy

No cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, analytics, account, service worker, advertising, or API calls. Answer history exists only in JavaScript memory. A refresh or new page resets it. Downloads and copied prompts happen only after your action. The site’s host may still keep ordinary web access logs; links to NCDOT or GitHub take you to their sites.

Help make the bank stronger

Use “Flag this question” to prepare a local issue report. Repository contributors should check the source-review queue, read the authoring instructions, preserve exceptions, and validate the bank before publishing. Routine structural tests do not certify road-law or emergency advice.
